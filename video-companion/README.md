### Source covers and future selection

Each playable idea uses the official publisher's video thumbnail, matched to its verified video ID. Small cover images are served as protected same-origin assets; `site/posters/provenance.json` records the publisher URL and hash. These are covers for the source, not claimed stills from the reviewed moment. No source video or full transcript is stored. An explicit Watch action opens the bounded moment. During playback, the app's feedback controls tuck away; tap Controls or the caption to reveal them. The source player retains its own controls. The full conversation remains a separate optional context link.

Future curation should seek more concise primary media, news and explainers alongside leader interviews, retaining broad AI trends, opinions and capabilities. Source length alone is not a quality filter: every published moment still needs a complete reviewed 30–90s idea, verified original date, attribution and context. Increase the number of qualified ideas when evidence allows; do not fill the feed with weak or unverified material.

A new video ID also needs a matched publisher cover and provenance before release. `npm run validate` checks every playable card has a cover and verifies its recorded source URL and file hash; missing or substituted covers block publication. The reviewed editorial updater remains limited to content and its application ledger.
